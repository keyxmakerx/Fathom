In one sentence: the forty-spoke picture was unreadable at under 600 SVG elements — two orders of magnitude below 44 §4.7.4's 2,000-element cap — so that cap is a performance ceiling and the corpus has never written down the legibility ceiling that actually bites; this variant sets it at six like-kind siblings, collapses on that, and then spends the one channel A-schematic refused on the one meaning the field card itself groups by — the security zone.

1 · The aggregation finding, and the threshold

44 §4.7.4 decides the diagram “never renders more than 2,000 live SVG elements” and aggregates above that; 52 §3.6.1 repeats it as an IA consequence; 56 §12 carries the st0 fan as an OPEN decision needing “a design pass and a hub fixture”. This is that pass, and it found the rule stated in the wrong unit. A-schematic's fixture at 40 spokes measures 587 elements and is already a texture. At 120 it measures 807 — still 60% below the cap — and the fit zoom falls to 0.23, under the 0.35 level-of-detail floor, so the picture degrades to boxes and rails with no labels at all while remaining comfortably inside the element budget. A ceiling that never fires is not a ceiling.

44 §4.7.4's 2,000 is a performance ceiling on TOTAL elements. The legibility ceiling is a separate number, it is counted per LIKE-KIND SIBLING SET rather than per scene, and it is two orders of magnitude smaller. Both are needed: the element cap stops the browser dying, the sibling cap stops the reader dying. Only the second one ever fires on a real estate.

The threshold is six, and it is six because two independent arguments land on the same number:

  • Perceptual. Enumeration without counting — subitising — is exact to four and gone by six. Six is the last count a reader can still verify by pairing rather than by counting one at a time. Above it you are asking a human to do arithmetic the drawing could have done for them, and it will be done wrong.
  • Geometric, and it falls out of this layout's own constants. Six units at G.portPitch (16px) is 96px, which is exactly G.pitch, the rank pitch. A sibling set that outgrows one rank pitch has stopped being a property of its node and become a structure in its own right — and structures get drawn once, with a count, not n times.

Concept 03 in round one chose eight and the corpus never contradicted it; six is tighter and it is derived rather than picked. The consequence is visible in the controls: at 4 spokes nothing collapses at all — the aggregated and un-aggregated renders are identical at 228 elements — and the machinery only appears when the picture actually needs it. That is the test of a threshold: it must be invisible on a small estate.

The expansion page is twelve = 2 × the threshold, and 12 × 16px = 192px = G.maxW, the widest box the width ladder allows, so one expanded page is at most one box tall. An explicit request tolerates more than a default does; it does not tolerate 120. Expanding a 120-spoke bundle shows twelve and a counted, focusable +108 more continuation — because expanding 120 into 120 is the texture again with extra steps.

2 · What collapses, and what it says while collapsed

  • Unit level. st0.0…st0.39 become one stub inside the SRX reading st0.0–39 · +40.
  • Peer level. SPOKE-01…40 become one ExternalPeer box reading SPOKE-01–40 over 40 spokes ▸.
  • Member level. ae0's members collapse to xe-0/0/2–17 · +16 with the edge label ae0 · 16 members. Two members never reach the threshold, so the LAG member count is a control on this page — a collapse rule you cannot exercise is a collapse rule you have not tested.
  • Port level is the same code path. Aggregation is a property of a port group on a side, so any side of any node crossing the threshold collapses by the same mechanism. In this fixture only the hub's right wall does.

The unit stub and the peer box share one expansion state, deliberately. They are the same forty objects seen from two ends — the 1:1:1 of unit, conduit and peer — and there is no useful state in which forty units fan into one box. Both are separately focusable and separately announced; they just tell the truth about the same set. Expansion is otherwise partial: expanding the tunnel bundle does not expand ae0, because a reader following one path asked about one thing. Global expansion exists as its own control, show every one, and it is there so the two forms can be put side by side.

Nothing is ever hidden without a count, and the count is the one label in the picture that may not be demoted. Every other label here can be counted out by the collision search or dropped by the level-of-detail ladder. A count cannot: aggregate labels claim their boxes before anything else is placed, are exempt from the LOD gate, and are drawn even if every candidate position collides. Six carriers say the same number at once — the stub label, the box's second line, the conduit's own label, the view band's lead (diagram · 10 nodes · 15 edges · 40 tunnels collapsed, which is G10 / 56 §5.2's release valve), the Outline row, and the accessible name.

3 · Colour: one meaning, permanently

The hue channel carries the security zone or domain and nothing else — because that is what the field card itself groups by (security-zone VPN, security-zone WAN, from-zone TRUST to-zone VPN) and it is what an engineer traces when following a path. Five domains, all real in corpus/commands/junos-srx-ipsec.yaml: TRUST, VPN, MGMT, WAN, UNTRUST. Zone bands take the hue, node boundaries take the hue, and an edge takes the hue of its zone. Everything else — the site region, the VLAN bracket, the RI box, chassis rules, junctions, crossings, plates, annotation, selection and focus — stays exactly as neutral as A-schematic left it. 18.6% of the SVG elements carry a hue.

Three rules make it a design rather than a paint job:

  • A terminal carries the zone of its own interface, not of the box it lands on. On a firewall those differ, and that difference is the firewall. So the SRX's reth0 stubs are WAN, its fxp0 is MGMT, its ge-0/0/4–5 are TRUST and its st0.* are VPN — and each of those five terminals prints its zone next to its interface name.
  • A boundary device is computed, not declared, and keeps the neutral outline. Any node whose terminals span two or more zones is a boundary device — it is in no zone because it is the thing that defines them. It prints BOUNDARY. Neutral therefore still has exactly one meaning in this picture.
  • An edge that changes zone changes tone at the zone boundary. The split is put on the zone band's own rectangle and the geometry is cut there, not faded. Two edges do this in the fixture — WAN → UNTRUST at the ISP and TRUST → VPN on the policy edge — and the view band counts them.

Evidence age outranks zone. A stale or unverified box keeps its --muted outline and loses its hue, because “this evidence is sixteen months old” is a stronger thing to say about a box than “it is in TRUST”. Likewise an inferred edge stays --muted and a proposed edge stays dashed: provenance beats zone, product-wide, and the precedence is written into the cascade rather than into a comment.

4 · The reservation, kept — and the redundancy, proved

ADR-0011 invariant 11 keeps green, amber and red for the risk enum permanently. The five zone hues are measured at HSL 272°, 251°, 228°, 208° and 198° — violet through indigo and blue to cyan. Green runs 90–150°, amber 30–60°, red wraps 330–20°. The nearest approach of any hue here to any reserved family is 48° (UNTRUST to the green boundary). No topology object in this file resolves to --safe, --caution or --danger, at any layer, in any state.

WCAG 1.4.1 says colour may never be the sole carrier. Two independent non-chromatic carriers ride with every hue, and the page ships a control to check it.

  • Value. The five strokes are luminance-ordered by distance from the protected inside, so filter: grayscale(1) converts hue into an ordered value ramp rather than destroying it. Greyscale bytes are 47 · 58 · 69 · 80 · 92, evenly spaced 11–12 apart, every one strictly between --ink (23) and --muted (101) so no zone hue can be misread as ink or as de-emphasis. The washes are isoluminant with --surface by construction — 243, 244, 244, 244, 243 against 244 — so in greyscale the tonal ladder collapses back to A-schematic's exact three neutral steps.
  • Text. Every zone name is printed: on the band (zone TRUST), on every node (a right-aligned tag on the name row), and on every terminal of the boundary device. Delete the entire proposal block and the picture still says which zone everything is in.

A finding the drawing forced. A security zone is a set of interfaces, not a region of the picture. Zone VPN owns the st0 units inside the SRX and every peer those units reach, and those live in different columns — one band cannot enclose both without swallowing the core layer. So VPN is drawn as two bands with the same name. That is exactly why the per-node printed tag is not optional: enclosure alone cannot carry a discontiguous set, so a design that leaned on the band as the non-chromatic carrier would have a hole in it precisely where zones get interesting.

Forced colours and print. Under forced-colors: active every zone token collapses to CanvasText / Canvas and the box stroke resolves to the system stroke — measured, not asserted — while all ten zone tags stay drawn. That is the test that the redundancy is real, and this variant deliberately reverses A-schematic's forced-color-adjust: none on the diagram classes: a neutral tonal ladder is worth defending from the UA, a hue channel is not. @media print forces the zone tags on and exempts them from the LOD ladder, because a mono printer erases every hue in the file.

5 · Does it look better, or just busier?

Better in one specific way, busier in another, and the honest answer depends on which question the reader arrived with.

Better: the picture now has a second readable structure. In A-schematic the only grouping visible at a glance is rank — what is above what. Here the zone partition is legible before you read a single label, and the two genuinely interesting facts about this estate are exactly the two the hue makes loud: the SRX is the only neutral box in a coloured picture (it is the boundary), and precisely two edges change tone (they are the only two that cross a boundary). Both were already true in A-schematic and both were invisible. Flip monochrome above and count how long it takes to find them.

Busier: the washes add a fourth and fifth tonal region inside the site region, which at 40 spokes means the lower two thirds of the picture is a large lavender field. The zone bands were always there; tinting them makes them assert themselves at a distance where previously they receded. And the tags are the real cost — see below.

The load-bearing worry, stated plainly: hue is being spent on a nominal partition of the nodes, which is information the bands already carry geometrically and the tags already carry textually. It buys speed, not information. That is a real purchase — a glance is worth a lot — but it is not a new fact, and it is the whole of the case.

6 · The two costs of ALWAYS-ON, said out loud

Cost one: the channel is spent permanently and cannot be reallocated. This is the difference between this variant and an overlay model, and it is not a small one. Once zone owns hue, hue can never mean utilisation, health, VLAN, routing-instance, blast radius, change-set membership, or “the six devices this command is about to touch”. Every one of those is a plausible future ask; the last is close to Fathom's actual purpose. An always-on model answers the question the designer picked in advance and refuses every later one. A diagram is a long-lived artefact and this is a one-way door.

Cost two: the risk legend is a few hundred pixels away, and yes, they compete — but not for meaning. Look at agg-A4-withlegend.png, or at the Risk panel to the right of the picture on this page. The three reserved chips are green, amber and red; the picture is violet, indigo, blue and cyan; nothing in the diagram is within 48° of a reserved family and no reader will confuse a TRUST switch with a safe command. The semantic collision the reservation exists to prevent does not happen.

What does happen is an attentional collision, and it runs the opposite way from the one you would expect. The three risk chips are the only warm, high-saturation marks on an otherwise cool page, so the eye goes to them first even though they are three 32px swatches against a 976px drawing. Beside a monochrome diagram the risk legend was the only colour on the page and therefore self-evidently the most important thing on it. Beside a coloured diagram it is still the most salient thing, but it now reads as one of several colour systems rather than as the colour system — the reservation is defended semantically and diluted rhetorically. If “what does red mean here” has to survive being learned once and trusted forever, that dilution is the real price, and this variant pays it on every screen, permanently, whether or not the reader cares about zones today.

8 · What it cost, measured

  • The zone tag costs 160px of scene width — 19.6%. At 40 spokes the scene is 976 × 636 with tags and 816 × 636 without; the tag enters the width formula, so five boxes climb a step on the {96,128,160,192} ladder. That is the entire price of colour independence and it is paid on every render. It is not all loss: the wider boxes give the label placer more room and hidden labels drop from 8 to 6.
  • Colour itself costs zero elements. 210 with hue, 210 without, at 40 spokes — the split geometry is emitted either way and the class does the painting, so monochrome is provably the same drawing. The washes are 6 rects and the splits 2 extra paths; both exist in both modes.
  • Aggregation costs 3 focusable groups and 2 doubled outlines, and saves 377 elements at 40 spokes and 597 at 120.
  • Two bands now carry the name zone VPN. Correct, and it will read as a duplicate to anyone who has not been told that a zone is a set of interfaces. A band-level “1 of 2” badge would fix it and is not built.
  • The security layer gates the hue. With sec out of the projection there are no zones to mean anything, so the picture returns to monochrome and the view band says zone colour off — security layer out of the projection. Principled, and still a surprise the first time you hit it.

9 · Where this does not hold up

The threshold is a constant, not a function of the pane. Six is right for a 640–900px pane at fit zoom. On a 4K wall display twelve stacked units are perfectly readable and six is over-eager; on a phone, four would already be too many. The honest version reads the rendered pitch in screen pixels — the same discipline A-schematic already applies to the conduit gap and the twin rail — and collapses when a sibling set would fall below the readable pitch. That is a small change and it is not built here.

Group membership is declared, not derived. The two groups are found by n.lateral and by e.members. A real implementation has to detect like-kind sibling sets from the graph — same kind, same neighbour, same layer, interchangeable — and that classifier is where this design would actually get hard. In particular I have not answered what happens when forty spokes are not alike: if six are stale and one is unverified, collapsing them hides an age distinction that 55 would very much like kept. The right answer is probably that a collapsed set inherits the worst age in it and says so, and that is neither built nor drawn.

Expansion re-runs the whole layout. Expanding the bundle changes the node count, which changes the peer stack, which changes the corridor, which re-centres every rank. The mental-map seeding in phase 5 keeps the ordering stable, but boxes do move, and 56 §3.5 is unambiguous that they should not. A proper implementation expands into reserved space.

Two labels are force-placed at 40 spokes with ae0 at 16. The never-demoted rule guarantees a count is drawn; it does not guarantee it is drawn somewhere pretty. ge-0/0/5 · TRUST and ae0 · 16 members sit within a few pixels of each other under the SRX because every candidate position collided. The rule is right — a forced overlap is better than a silent omission — but the candidate set needs to be larger before this ships.

UNTRUST and WAN are the weakest hue pair. 198° against 208°, greys 92 and 80. They are adjacent in both channels because the palette is a monotone ramp in hue and in value, and a ramp's neighbours are always its hardest pair. They are printed and they are bracketed, so nothing is lost — but if only two zones were ever going to be confused at a glance it is those two, and “the edge” against “beyond the edge” is not a distinction you want soft.

Everything A-schematic could not do, it still cannot do. x-assignment is still the priority method rather than Brandes–Köpf; multi-rank edges still run in a margin lane rather than through phase 4's dummy nodes; label placement is still fixed-side first; sub-pixel truth below 1:1 is still exact only at z = 1 and z = 2. This variant inherited those and did not fix them.
