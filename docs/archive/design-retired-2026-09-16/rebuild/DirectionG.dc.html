Because you selected a port
Left shows where the copper goes. Right shows what the interface means: VLAN 20, gateway for 10.10.20.0/24, zone trust. The thick line is the same cable, drawn as a Layer 3 hop.
What this gets you
The physical-versus-logical problem stops being a mode switch. You see the cable and the route at once, and the thing that joins them — the port — is what you click. Teaching falls out of it for free: the right pane explains the left.
What it costs
Half a screen each. Three layers means one is always hidden, and on a laptop both panes are cramped. Needs a strong "swap" and "maximise one" gesture.
Best if
The daily question is "what does this port do" or "where does this subnet physically live". That is a lot of network engineering.
