**Approved by the owner on 2026-11-02**, sign-off card r87-reader-packages: question 1 A (Fathom's
own SNMPv3 reader on RustCrypto pieces), question 2 A (russh 0.65.0 on ring, with RSA host keys)
and all three ticks (the rsa advisory, two release candidates, 145 packages with 16 build
scripts). The card measured the whole reader, serde_json included, in a scratch workspace that day.

The first part carried serde_json's closure for `ping`. The SNMPv3 part adds the RustCrypto pieces
(`hmac`, `sha1`, `sha2`, `aes`, `cfb-mode`) and `getrandom`, the set measured for r87 question 1
A; each direct one has its own record here. `ctutils` is held at `0.4.6`: `0.4.7` was published
on 29 Oct 2026, inside the seven-day cooldown, and the measured set had it at the same major.
russh arrives in the SSH part. The table is regenerated by the same script, never typed.

The reader is **its own workspace with its own lockfile**, so none of these reach Fathom's lock,
its gates or its server image, and the compose `reader` profile is what builds it. The gates run
over this lock in `scripts/gate-reader.sh` and CI's `reader` job.
