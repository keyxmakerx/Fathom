**Approved by the owner on 2026-11-02**, sign-off card r87-reader-packages: question 1 A (Fathom's
own SNMPv3 reader on RustCrypto pieces), question 2 A (russh 0.65.0 on ring, with RSA host keys)
and all three ticks (the rsa advisory, two release candidates, 145 packages with 16 build
scripts). The card measured the whole reader, serde_json included, in a scratch workspace that day.

The first part carried serde_json's closure for `ping`. The SNMPv3 part added the RustCrypto pieces
(`hmac`, `sha1`, `sha2`, `aes`, `cfb-mode`) and `getrandom`, the set measured for r87 question 1
A. The SSH part adds `russh` `0.65.0` with `ring` and `rsa` and no default features (no
`aws-lc-rs`, no compression), and `tokio`, the runtime russh already brings, with `rt`, `net` and
`time` only; each direct one has its own record here. `fathom-ingest`, the redaction gate, is a
path dependency on Fathom's own crate and adds nothing here.

script, for Linux).** The table below lists every package the lockfile holds for every target,
183, including the Windows, WebAssembly and Android crates `cargo` resolves but never builds for
the reader's image. Built for `x86_64-unknown-linux-gnu` (`cargo metadata --filter-platform
x86_64-unknown-linux-gnu`, normal and build edges from the reader, registry packages only,
3 Nov 2026) the reader is **144 packages, 15 with a build script**: inside the approved
measurement on both counts. Two duplicates remain under ring (`getrandom` 0.2 and, on Windows
only, `windows-sys` 0.52) and one at build time (`syn` 2 under three proc macros); each is
skipped by name and version in `deny.toml`, with its reason.

Held back for the seven-day cooldown, 3 Nov 2026: `cc` at `1.10.0` (1.11.0 published 2 Nov),
`log` at `0.4.34` (0.4.35, 30 Oct), and `js-sys`, `wasm-bindgen` and its macro, macro-support and

its gates or its server image, and the compose `reader` profile is what builds it. The gates run
