Concept 05 — the paired ledger · thesis, costs and seams · study apparatus, not part of the product

Thesis, one sentence

Every fact the product holds is posted twice — once as structure on the left, once as meaning on the right — and where the second posting is missing the ledger says so out loud instead of pretending the row is complete.

The layout, three sentences

The sheet is 52 §2.2's furniture unchanged — 3px rule, masthead, legend, view band, footer — and the body below it is permanently two columns: fact at 62% and meaning at 38%, never one, never three. The left column is whichever of the six views you are in; the right column is the explainer layer (52 §4b, the margin drawer) with its pin welded shut. Moving the cursor on the left posts the matching entry on the right in the same frame, and the right column's header repeats the left row verbatim as a posting reference, so you never lose which row the meaning belongs to.

What it optimises for, and what it therefore sacrifices

It optimises for co-visibility of evidence and explanation, which is the one thing 52 §4.2 says an explainer must never make you trade. It sacrifices, in order:

  • The second view. 52 §2.3 allows exactly one second surface and says comparing two views side by side costs the inspector. The Ledger spends that surface permanently on meaning, so ⌥P pinning config beside findings is gone. That is the largest single cost and it is not recoverable inside this direction.
  • Adjacency. 52 §4.2 wanted the explainer directly under the thing, pushing content down. Here it is beside it. For row 3 that is the same thing; for row 40 the meaning appears at the top of the right column while the fact is at the bottom of the left, and the eye has to travel. The posting reference in the meaning header is the mitigation, not a cure.
  • Horizontal room at 1280px. 1180px of sheet at 62/38 gives the fact column ~700px and the meaning column ~430px. A 72-column mono config line needs about 620px, so config fits and the diagram does not get the room 56 wants. ⌥[ gives 50/50 for reading and ⌥] returns to 62/38; there is no draggable splitter, because a dragged ratio is state nobody remembers setting.
  • Below --bp-cols (860px) the coupling stops being spatial. The ledger stacks: meaning becomes a band under the fact column, capped at half the body. Resize this window and watch it happen — the breakpoint is read from the token file at runtime, not typed into a media query. 52 §2.4 would simply refuse the split below 1100px; refusing it here would remove the product's thesis, so it degrades instead of disappearing, and that is a deliberate departure from 52.

How it serves teaching (goal a)

Teaching is not a view you visit; it is 38% of every screen you are ever on. The right column carries the corpus's three depths — three texts, not three lengths (15 §4) — controlled by a lowercase margin tab in the column head, never a settings screen, cycled with v or ⌥\. Every posting carries the ADR-0027 verification stamp, which for this corpus reads junos-srx · unverified, because the seed's own header says nothing in it has been run on a box. Selecting a config line, a finding, a diagram node or an inventory cell for the same object posts the same meaning, because the posting is keyed on the element id and not on the rendering (invariant 7).

How it serves inventory and diagramming (goal b)

Inventory is the left column at ⌥6: a table whose columns are generated from schema.yaml (toggle them in the strip above the table), whose rightmost column is not a field but the per-row finding aggregate, and under which the structural observations sit in the note idiom with two actions. Diagram is ⌥5 with independently toggled layers. The estate is a fixture derived from the field card's worked topology and is labelled as one in the masthead, on the table, and in a note — no inventory fixture ships in the corpus and inventing one silently is the one dishonesty this design system has no device for.

Moving between the two goals

The connection matrix is walkable in the page: a finder result takes w into the walkthrough that builds it; answering the walkthrough's PFS step writes the graph and removes a finding from the band count while you watch; a finding takes f into config·change set with the remediation, the verification ladder and the rollback; an inventory row's opinions cell takes you to findings filtered to that node; a diagram node highlights its emit closure in config. A view switch never changes the selection — it changes how the selection is drawn — and never scrolls the view you left.

Where new functionality plugs in — the seam, concretely

Drawn on screen in the findings view, with the words on the page: THIS IS WHERE A NEW RULE PACK PLUGS IN. Below the findings table is a packs table — id, version, signer, rules, last review — showing fathom.ipsec installed and unsigned, and fathom.mpls present in the trust store but not installed, drawn with a dotted rule because dotted means pending and dashed is reserved for AI. The finding mtu.mss-clamp.absent sits in the same list as ipsec.pfs.absent and renders identically, which is the point of invariant 5: a new finding is a YAML document and zero lines of code. Two further seams are visible rather than claimed: every emitted config line's provenance expansion (M9) is what makes "click any line to learn what it does" a consequence of the architecture rather than a feature; and the inventory's column strip is M7's generated column picker.

The constraint on extension, stated so it is clear it was understood: new functionality lands as a mode of an existing view, a layer that opens inside all of them, a diagram layer, an inventory kind, or a rule pack — never as a seventh tab. The Ledger makes that easier to obey than most shells, because the meaning column is already a layer that opens inside all six.

How the user can tell it cannot phone home

Press the no sockets, ever margin tab, top right. The meaning column posts the artifact itself: the CSP in this page's own head, the two-entry WASM import section, the two ship gates (X0.8 asserts connect-src 'none' against the final bytes, X0.9 fails the build on any connection attempt in a 30-minute proxied session), the corpus as zstd frames inside the artifact, the base64 fonts, and the absence of browser storage. It is a two-column hairline table — the same table the error decoder uses — because a claim you can read row by row is checkable and a badge is not. There is no green shield, no lock, no trust score and no use of --safe: that colour means read-only and reusing it for "offline" would be the single most visible failure a concept could make.

The absence is structural elsewhere too: no status dot, no last-updated, no refresh control, no spinner, no progress bar, no toast. --z-egress: 10 is never allocated by this page — a layer the product declares and never uses, which is its own statement.

The strongest argument against this direction

It spends 38% of every screen, forever, on a column that is empty or near-empty most of the time — and the phase plan says it will stay that way for a year. Under ADR-0006 v1 is the finder alone: no graph, no config, no findings, no inventory, no diagram. In that product the left column has one view and the right column has real corpus text, so the Ledger looks excellent. The moment the graph arrives — 24–34 solo weeks for phase 1, 14–20 more for phase 2 — the left column fills with nodes the corpus has no explainer for, and the right column starts reading UNPOSTED on row after row. The ledger's honesty device becomes its dominant visual state. A reviewer can reasonably say: you have built a layout whose main job is to display the absence of the content that justifies the layout.

Three weaker but real objections. (1) A 430px meaning column is below the card's own ~480px column, so teaching prose sets narrower than the artefact the language was extracted from. (2) Permanently spending the second surface means the two tasks 52 §2.3 named as needing a split — drawing while watching config, walking while watching findings — cannot be done side by side at all; this concept can only offer them as a view switch. (3) The direction is least convincing exactly where the owner's second goal lives: a wide inventory table wants the whole 1180px, and the Ledger will not give it, and "sort by the opinions column and read across" is a worse experience here than in a full-width table. The honest answer to (3) is that ⌥[ and a narrower window are the only relief on offer, and neither is a fix.

The phase boundary, made legible rather than resolved

The view band marks corpus-backed views and graph-backed views differently, and the footer keeps a running balance of postings. Finder, the explainer depths and the risk legend are real today. Config, findings, diagram, inventory and the walkthrough are drawn against a fixture and are 50–72 solo weeks past the artifact ADR-0006 says ships first. The owner brief is the authority and ADR-0006 is Accepted; they disagree, and this page's job is to make the disagreement cheap to look at, not to pick a side.
