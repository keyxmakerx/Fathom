Concept 04 — the console

esc closes
Thesis
Fathom is a closed vocabulary of verbs over one graph and one corpus, and its transcript is its audit log — so what this tool can do and what this tool just did are the same list, printed on the screen, and neither list contains a verb that opens a socket.
Optimises for
Speed for the returning user and legibility of capability for the sceptical one. Hands stay on home row; every state change is named, ordered and reviewable; the second surface is a ledger rather than an inspector, so the session is a document you can read back.
Sacrifices
Three things, named. (1) The ledger occupies the one second surface (52 §2.3, R35/ADR-0025), so this concept spends the inspector: provenance opens inline in the canvas and pushes content down, and you cannot have the ledger and a pinned second view at once. (2) The command line and the suggestion strip cost about 44px of the body permanently, on top of the ~210px of sheet furniture — that is real rent for a control the expert stops reading. (3) Below 1100px the ledger is gone, and with it the concept's whole security argument; the fallback is the provenance mode of the same pane, which is not visible when the pane is not.
Teaching
The console's usual failure is command not found. Here the parse failure is the teaching path: anything the vocabulary does not recognise falls through to the finder as a corpus query, and the meta slot tells you which it will be before you press Enter (→ view versus → find (corpus)). Explainers are a layer, not a view: three depths on a margin tab, opened inline under the thing they explain, never overlaying the evidence. e works on a finder result, a config line, a finding — and on a ledger row, so you can ask the transcript why a verb did what it did.
Inventory and diagramming
Inventory columns are generated from the schema (11 §11.6) — switch kind with kind IkeGateway and the columns change without a line of view code. The rightmost column is not a field: it is the per-row finding aggregate, so you cannot look at the estate without seeing what the rule engine thinks of it. Below the table sit the population rules in the note idiom, with actions that argue back. The diagram is a design tool, not a source of truth: layers toggle inline, nodes carry their provenance and age, and selecting one lights its emit closure in config.
Where new functionality plugs in
Two seams, both drawn on the page rather than claimed. The rule pack (M2 / 63 §2): open findings and read the pack strip — fathom.ipsec 0.4.0 and fathom.mtu 0.3.0, each with signer, rule count and review date, and each finding row attributed to its pack. mtu.mss-clamp.absent is sitting in the list beside ipsec.pfs.absent and it comes from a different signed bundle. The verb table (M5 / M10): open the ledger's vocabulary mode. Every verb carries a source — shell, view:findings, mode:config.changeset, pack:fathom.mtu — so a new mode, a new layer or a new pack adds rows to a table rather than a tab to the band. A seventh view is not an extension point and this concept has no room for one, which is the design working.
How you can tell it cannot phone home
Not by a badge. By three checks you can run yourself, printed in the ledger's provenance mode: read the CSP in this file's own <head>; grep the script for the four call sites that could originate a request; run it with the cable out. The structural version is the vocabulary table — the verb list is the capability list, its network column reads — on every row, and the shipped core's WASM import section is two entries neither of which can reach a socket (41 §3.7, 34 §7.5). The counter in the ledger header is not a measurement; it is a property. There is no green shield, and there never will be: green means read-only.
The strongest argument against
This is the direction most likely to be loved by its author and abandoned by its audience. 53 §1 says group C — text entry — decides the scheme, and this concept adds a second text-entry surface that competes with the finder for the same keystrokes and the same muscle memory: Ctrl+K and : both open a box you type into, and the honest question is why there are two. The suggestion strip mitigates the blank prompt but does not remove it, and a strip that shows the six most relevant of twelve verbs is a menu that has been un-labelled. The ledger is the sharpest cost: it is a genuinely excellent security surface and a mediocre working surface, and every hour of real use it spends 38% of the width on a list the user has already read. A reviewer who says "make the ledger a mode of the footer and give the second surface back to the inspector" is not wrong, and if this direction is chosen that is the first change to argue about.
Phase boundary
Stated, not resolved. ADR-0006 decides v1 is phase 0 alone — v1 = the finder, nothing about a graph. The owner has since named inventory and diagramming as goal (b), and that is not one of ADR-0006's own revisit triggers. Both are in force and they disagree. On this page the provenance mode carries the split: which surfaces are corpus-backed today (finder, explainers, the risk legend, the verification stamp) and which need the graph (config, findings, diagram, inventory, walkthrough), with 71 §2's own week counts. Phases 0+1+2 is 50–72 solo weeks against v1's 12–18, and 83 §12.5 found 71's inputs low by 1.5–1.6×. Treat it as a floor.
