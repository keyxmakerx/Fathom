Motion that means something
Your rule is already on file: motion must carry meaning. Five animations, each tied to a real event. Nothing moves to look alive.
1 · The cable droops as you pull itWhile dragging, the line hangs between the fixed port and your hand like a real lead, drooping more the further you pull. You feel the slack before you commit. Meaning: how much cable this run needs.
t=0 · grab port, short lead, barely bows
t=300ms · pulled further, the belly drops
t=600ms · over a free port: it snaps to the jack, target thickens
release · dashes fill solid, sag settles 80ms, length appears
2 · A wrong drop is refused, visiblyCopper onto a fibre cage, or an occupied port: the target shakes once, sideways, and the cable springs back into your hand. Never a silent nothing, never a modal. Meaning: that is not a legal cable.
t=0 · over an SFP cage, already dimmed as incompatible
t=40ms · dropped: cage jumps 6px right
t=80ms · 6px left
t=120–260ms · cage settles, lead springs back to your hand. Still dragging.
3 · The path lights up in orderHover any segment and the hops illuminate one after another from where you are, 60ms apart, out to both ends. The far end pans into view if it is off-screen. Meaning: this is one physical run, and this is its direction.
t=0 · hover the patch cord
+60ms · both end ports light, then the panel
+120ms · the riser run behind the panel
+180ms · far rack's port, and the viewport eases to include it
4 · A change of state pulses onceWhen a port's link state changes (later, when monitoring exists), its dot expands once and settles at the new state. It never blinks, never loops. Meaning: something just happened here — you did not miss it, and it is not still happening.
steady · link up
t=0–200ms · ring expands once from the dot
t=400ms · ring fades out, dot now hollow = down
steady · new state, no motion. Colour is not used; risk colours stay reserved.
5 · Zoom is one camera, not a page changeCloset → rack → faceplate → port is a single continuous move. Ports fade in as they become big enough to hit. You never lose which rack, which U. Meaning: where you are. The thing the zoom-canvas idea got right, kept; the getting-lost, removed by the rail.
z=25% · the closet. Devices are bars.
z=70% · the rack. Ports appear as dots, fading in.
z=200% · the faceplate. Jacks, numbered.
z=600% · one port. Its facts sit beside it.
Not on this list, on purpose: particles flowing along links. UniFi does it and its own users complain the animation runs ahead of the data. If it moves, it must be true right now.
