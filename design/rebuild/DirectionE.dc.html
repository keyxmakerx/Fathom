Read across, then down
The cable from jack 2-114 carries VLAN 20, which lives in 10.10.20.0/24, whose gateway is the firewall. The firewall is the only place the path changes zone, so it is the only place a policy decides anything. Everything to the left is switching; everything to the right is routing.
What Fathom will not say
It does not say permitted or denied. It names the zones, the policy set between them, and the policies in the order the device reads them. The verdict is yours; the evidence is laid out.
What this gets you
The question engineers actually ask — why does this go there — answered in one picture, with physical, L2 and L3 aligned instead of on three screens. The trace is already fully specified in the engine; this is the first face that draws it.
What it costs
It is a way of reading an estate, not building one. On its own it cannot be the whole product — it needs a drawing surface underneath to pick endpoints from.
Where I would put it
Not as the shell. As the teaching mode inside whichever shell wins — and the thing the config checker opens when you click a flagged policy.
