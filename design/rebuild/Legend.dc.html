1
Ports
Same size on every plate. Filled = cabled, hollow = free. Count and position come from the engine's catalogue, never typed.
2
Lines
Type is the construction, never the hue. Every cable sags right and down; longer vertical runs sag more, capped.
3
Two palettes
Plastic is a line. Ink is a box.
That is the whole rule. A sheath colour is only ever a cable stroke. A risk colour is only ever a bordered wash with words in it. They can share a hue and never be confused.
Plastic · sheath · the lead in your hand · no dark-theme variant, because plastic does not change colour when the lights go off
Ink · risk · reserved · adapts to dark
read-only changes config disruptive
The closest pair, side by side
Risk never fills a cable or a port. A sheath never fills a region, a border or a word. Where a risk fact sits on the canvas — an unreachable policy, weak auth on a routing peer — it is drawn as a bordered wash, never as bare coloured text.
4
Presence
No cursors. A ring is where someone's hands are. A chip is who is in the room. Scoped to the view.
5
Portals
Not either/or. The cable runs to the edge and the tray labels the crossing.
Rule text lives in docs/UI-SPEC.md. Tokens in design/tokens.css. This board is the picture of both; when they disagree, the text wins and this board is wrong. black = ink · a virtual link inside a box has no sheath and draws in muted, taking a colour only at the jack where it becomes a cable
