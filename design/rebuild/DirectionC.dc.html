What this gets you
Almost no chrome. One mental model: everything is somewhere, and you go there. Scales to any depth — campus, NOC, closet, port — without new screens.
What it costs
The hardest to build and the easiest to get lost in. Comparing two distant things is awkward. Needs excellent "where am I" cues or it feels like falling.
