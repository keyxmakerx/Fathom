What this gets you
Closets, NOCs, sublet floors, risers, who owns what — everything you listed is a place, and this puts place first. A new engineer can find the switch because they can find the room. Ownership is literally hatched on the floor.
What it costs
Heavy on physical, lighter on logical. VLANs and subnets are overlays, which is right, but a pure L3 question (two sites, no buildings) needs a different canvas. And someone has to draw the rooms.
Best if
Most of the pain is physical: which closet, which riser, which rack, whose kit. For an office estate, that is most of the pain.
