One change here is worth a look.
On 06 Sep the policy allow-web went from matching one application to matching any. That is the difference between a web rule and an open door. Click to see before and after side by side.
What this gets you
Nobody else does this, and you are uniquely able to: the sealed history means every past state is real, not reconstructed. "What changed since Friday" becomes the first thing you see. Audit, blame, rollback and why is this broken now all fall out of it.
What it costs
It is not a drawing-first interface, and drawing is your first priority. On day one there is no history to show. It is a killer mode, not a killer shell.
Where I would put it
A scrubber that can be pulled down over any other direction — and the default landing view once an estate is a few weeks old.
