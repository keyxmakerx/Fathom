What this gets you
Everyone already understands layers — it is how every drawing tool and every map works. Physical and Layer 3 are the same picture seen differently, which is true of a network and is the thing most tools get wrong.
What it costs
Only shows one place at a time. Comparing two closets, or following a circuit from HQ to a branch, means leaving and coming back.
Best if
Most work is "I am in one closet, show me everything about it."
