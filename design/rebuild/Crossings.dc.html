Forty cables, and you can still read it
Your complaint with the building view was a single line crossing others and hiding that it was many. These four techniques, together, are how the rack never gets there. Same six devices in every panel.
1 · Bundlesat rest
Nineteen cables. Four lines. Nothing crosses that you cannot count.
2 · Fan on hoverpointer over the ×8 band
Which of the eight is port 15? Now you can see. Move away and it folds back.
3 · Light the whole pathhover one segment
A cable is never "a line". It is a path, and the path is what you point at.
4 · Toggle by kind, colour by leadpower on, sheath colours on
Copper, fibre and power toggle independently. Colour is the real sheath — the lead you actually used — so a cable in your hand can be found on screen. Type is carried by the line, not the hue: copper one stroke, fibre a pair, power heavy.
