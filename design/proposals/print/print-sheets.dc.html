A drawing wider than a page is cut into pages at 1:1 rather than shrunk until nothing reads. Each page carries corner marks and a dashed overlap line; what lies past the line prints on both neighbours so the pages can be laid together. A link that leaves the page says where it goes. Type is still the line: the fibre to the Garage is a pair, the copper a single stroke, so black and white loses nothing.
