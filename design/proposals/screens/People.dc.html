What a seconding is
A fresh signature from a steward who is neither the granter nor the subject. Until one arrives the grant is inert. Seconding is a touch of your key, not a row in a table.
Nothing on this trail can be edited or removed. A withdrawal would be a new sealed entry that reverses this one.
Operators cannot grant. Stewards do, and every grant is signed. Every name, address, date and capability on this board is a sample.
