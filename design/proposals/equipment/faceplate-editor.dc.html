Rules at this level
The plate is the size you set: U height and full or half width for rack gear, width × height × depth in mm for a free-standing box. Ports snap to a grid at a third of the pitch the rack draws them at, so a drawn box and a catalogue box sit alike. Dragging a connector on adds a port to fw-01 on this face; the drawing adds only its position. A group (four SFP+ cages, twelve RJ45) is one thing, placed together and laid out by a preset. A slot holds a part; its inlet is the port. A mark is only a mark: it never takes a cable.
